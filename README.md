# How to Delete Your Account and Data

Since **My Pigeon PH** is an offline-first app, your pigeon records, loft data, and pedigree details are stored locally on your device, while minimal account and device metadata (`users` and `devices`) is kept in Cloud Firestore.   

You have two ways to remove your account, but **if you have already deleted your account directly inside the app, there is no need to submit a request through the web portal.**

---

### Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended & Instant)
If you delete your account directly through the app settings:
* Your cloud account profile and device binding metadata in Firestore are permanently removed immediately.
* **No further portal deletion request is needed**, as your data is already fully cleared from our servers.

---

### Option 2: Request via Web Portal or Email
If you are unable to access the app and need to request cloud data removal manually:
1. Contact developer support or visit our portal using your registered email address (`mypigeonph@gmail.com`).
2. Once processed, your **User Profile (`users`)** and **Device Bindings (`devices`)** stored in Firestore will be permanently purged.   

---

### What About Local Device Data & Files?
Because the app operates offline:
* Deleting your online account (either in-app or via portal) **does not** automatically wipe local app data or files saved on your phone.
* **To remove local app data:** Uninstall the **My Pigeon PH** app from your phone or clear app data via your Android settings (**Settings > Apps > My Pigeon PH > Storage > Clear Data**).
* **To remove saved exports and media:** Manually delete your local files:
  * Backup files (`.mypigeonph` or `.json`), **CSV files**, **PDF Genetics Certificates**, and **Fancier's Monthly Records** in your phone's **Downloads** or document folders.
  * Exported pigeon cards saved in your **Photos/Gallery** app.

---
